Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 7, 2026  ·  Last updated: April 7, 2026

Nudge ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when you use the Nudge iOS app.

1. Information We Collect

Nudge is designed to store your data on your device. We collect the minimum information needed to provide the service:

  • Goal and progress data — titles, notes, action items, and completion history you enter into the app. This is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.
  • Profile information (optional) — your name, age range, occupation, and interests that you choose to provide to personalize AI-generated nudges. This is stored locally on your device.
  • Nudge messages — AI-generated motivational messages are created by sending anonymized goal context to our Cloudflare Worker proxy, which forwards the request to Anthropic's Claude API. We do not log or store the content of these requests beyond what Anthropic retains under their own privacy policy.
  • Subscription status — managed entirely by Apple via StoreKit. We never see or store your payment information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display your goals, nudges, and progress within the app.
  • To generate personalized AI motivational messages via the Claude API.
  • To send optional push notifications for scheduled nudges.
  • To display a widget on your Home Screen or Lock Screen.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Third-Party Services

Nudge uses the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic Claude API — used to generate nudge messages. Goal context is sent to Anthropic's servers to produce a response. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare Workers — our proxy server that securely forwards requests to Anthropic without exposing our API key to the app. Cloudflare may collect standard server log data (IP address, timestamp). See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — handles all subscription purchases and receipts. We never receive your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

All goal and profile data is stored locally on your device. You can delete any or all data at any time by removing goals or uninstalling the app. We do not maintain a server-side copy of your personal data.

5. Push Notifications

Nudge may send push notifications to deliver scheduled motivational nudges. You can enable or disable notifications at any time in the app's Settings screen or in iOS Settings → Nudge → Notifications.

6. Children's Privacy

Nudge is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. International Users

If you access Nudge from outside the United States, your goal context may be processed by Anthropic and Cloudflare on servers located in the United States. By using the app, you consent to this transfer.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
